# Product Vision

## Vision
Make recovery decisions adaptive, measurable, and merchant-controlled.

## Mission
For every eligible failed payment, determine whether action is worthwhile and, if so, select the recovery intervention with the best expected value under merchant constraints.

## MVP Vision
A merchant can see revenue at risk, inspect a failed payment's context, understand the recommended intervention, approve or allow bounded execution, observe the outcome, and compare performance with a baseline.

## Long-Term Vision
RecoverAI can become a decision layer orchestrating multiple specialist recovery modules: failed payments, abandoned checkout, subscription recovery, receivables, and related revenue-risk workflows.

The broad AI Business Growth Strategist is future scope, not MVP scope.

## Product Principles
1. Context before action.
2. Expected value over blind automation.
3. Merchant-defined boundaries.
4. Measure incremental impact.
5. Explain every material decision.
6. Stop when action is no longer justified.
7. Fail safely and re-plan.
8. Do not use an LLM where deterministic logic is sufficient.

## Should Become
- Adaptive recovery decision/orchestration layer.
- Measurable revenue-recovery system.
- Merchant-controlled agentic workflow.

## Should Not Become
- Generic chatbot.
- Generic AI business assistant.
- Replacement for Razorpay payment infrastructure.
- Simple retry engine.
- Dashboard-only analytics product.
