# Competitive Analysis

## Razorpay Capabilities
**Strengths:** mature payment infrastructure; specialist recovery and agentic capabilities; payment links; MCP/API ecosystem.

**Gap relevant to RecoverAI:** the project focuses on adaptive, context-aware intervention selection and incremental measurement as a decision layer.

**RecoverAI position:** complement existing systems rather than replace them.

## Rule-Based Retry Systems
**Strengths:** simple, deterministic, predictable.
**Weaknesses:** limited contextual adaptation; may apply the same action too broadly.
**RecoverAI differentiation:** contextual candidate comparison plus probabilistic expected-value selection.

## Generic AI Automation
**Strengths:** flexible orchestration and language interfaces.
**Weaknesses:** can lack payment-specific constraints, attribution, and measurable recovery logic.
**RecoverAI differentiation:** payment-specific context, bounded actions, recovery metrics, and auditability.

## Shopify / Stripe / Adyen
Research identified strong agentic-commerce/payment infrastructure efforts across major providers. These demonstrate that generic agentic commerce infrastructure is crowded.

RecoverAI is deliberately narrower: post-failure revenue recovery decisioning rather than generic agentic shopping/checkout infrastructure.

## Important Positioning
Do not claim competitors lack recovery. The defensible statement is that RecoverAI targets a specific adaptive decision layer and demonstrates incremental impact.
