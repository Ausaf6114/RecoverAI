# Demo Flow

## 0:00–0:30 — Revenue at Risk
Show a batch of failed payments and revenue at risk.

## 0:30–1:15 — Context
Open one failed payment. Show payment amount, failure context, order and customer history.

## 1:15–2:00 — Decision
Show candidate interventions, estimated recovery probabilities/expected value, confidence, and selected action.

## 2:00–3:00 — Guardrail + Action
Merchant approval where required. Create a Standard Razorpay Payment Link in Test Mode and perform a test payment.

## 3:00–4:15 — Measurement
Show baseline versus RecoverAI recovered GMV, recovery rate, and incremental recovery on held-out/synthetic evaluation.

## 4:15–4:45 — Failure Handling
Inject a tool failure or already-completed-payment condition. Show safe stop/replan.

## 4:45–5:00 — Learning
Show predicted versus actual outcome and a segment/action learning update.

## Closing Line
“Razorpay already knows when a payment fails. RecoverAI decides what should happen next — and proves whether it recovered more money.”
