# Metrics and KPIs

## Primary KPI
Incremental Revenue Recovered.

## Secondary KPIs
- Revenue at risk
- Revenue recovered
- Recovery rate
- Recovery uplift
- Intervention success rate
- False intervention rate
- Policy violation rate (target: 0)
- Action execution success
- Outcome attribution rate
- Expected vs actual recovery
- ROI

## Evaluation
All final numbers must come from controlled evaluation or genuine test evidence. Never fabricate benchmark results.
