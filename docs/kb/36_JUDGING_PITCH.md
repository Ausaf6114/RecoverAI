# Judging Pitch

## Problem
Failed payments create revenue risk, but the right recovery action depends on context.

## Why Now
Payments and AI systems increasingly support agentic workflows, making bounded decision/orchestration layers practical.

## Why Razorpay
Razorpay provides the payment ecosystem and execution surfaces needed to demonstrate a real recovery workflow.

## Why AI
The intervention decision is uncertain and context-dependent.

## Why Agentic
RecoverAI observes a state, investigates, chooses an action, executes it, observes the result, and can re-plan.

## Why RecoverAI
It focuses on the measurable decision gap between failure and optimal intervention.

## Differentiation
Not a retry engine, chatbot, dashboard, rules-only system, or generic agent.

## Business Impact
The north-star is incremental recovered revenue versus baseline.

## Technical Innovation
Context aggregation + probabilistic intervention scoring + expected-value optimization + deterministic guardrails + real action execution + feedback.

## Demo Impact
Show actual Test Mode execution and measurable batch comparison.
